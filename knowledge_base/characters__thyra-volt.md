# Thyra Volt

Thyra Volt is a shade mage who is the youngest of the Nyx Coven, a trio of shade mages. Among the three, she is the most aggressive and self-confident, always believing herself to be the strongest out of her and her sisters (later retconned in Cycle 8 to being close friends), Virella Frost and Selka Shade. She was also a former student of Noctis Spire as she, Virella Frost and Selka Shade were expelled in the episode "Cold Spell."

Thyra Volt is a woman with a light beige skin tone, teal eyes, and frizzy dark purple hair that is shaped like a cloud with paler bangs which flow down to and rest on her shoulders in the shape of lightning bolts. A small hair can also been seen peeking out from the very top of her head. As for her makeup, Thyra Volt is always seen in dark pink eye shadow and lipstick.

In the episode "The Show Must Go On!" shows that Thyra Volt's hair reaches down to her shoulders when straightened.

Her attire consists of a fuchsia-colored crop-top with no straps as the only thing holding it up is its attachment to a matching fuchsia choker through the small metal ring that rests at the center of her top, a purple miniskirt that is tied to her waist (noted by the knot resting over a slit located at her left hip), purple romper-like shorts sometimes, and matching purple sandals with high heels.

Thyra Volt's various Shade Mage Forms come in shades of fuchsia, magenta and red; more so representing her anger and terrible temper than her powers.

As her name and powers would infer, Thyra Volt is a loud and wild young lady who is very quick to anger and rage. Even the most minor of annoyances usually lead to Thyra Volt violently lashing and, out of the three sisters, she is the master when it comes to holding grudges. Cases of this range from trying to settle the score from a previous battle by either attacking more violently or even disguising herself to catch the object of her vengeance off-guard. In fact, the latter case occurred only because Nyra Tune was able to dispel of her storms in the episode "Battle on Planet Dravenor," which damaged her pride since she believes herself to be the strongest. Despite how prideful she is, Thyra Volt is often tormented by Virella Frost whenever she gets the chance, thanks to her status as the youngest sibling. However, Thyra Volt makes it a habit to regularly harass her older sisters, mainly out of boredom.

Sharing her sisters' lust for power, Thyra Volt stands out as possibly the most power-hungry of the Nyx Coven, as she always remains more than eager to get her hands on more powerful magic. Coupled with her immaturity and pettiness, Thyra Volt often rushes head-first into situations to prove herself to be the "Queen of Storms" she titles herself as. Because of this, she lacks the craftiness of Selka Shade, or even Virella Frost. Thyra Volt has a lot of power, and uses hers in extreme bursts, without planning ahead at all. Thanks to her constant desire to prove that she is the strongest shade mage around, Thyra Volt rarely ever thinks ahead, preferring to charge in head-first and overwhelm her enemies with powerful and destructive attacks. She will even escalate small exchanges into a serious fight if she is annoyed enough.

True to her name, Thyra Volt possesses the ability to create and control storms in all of their aspects; ranging from harrowing winds, rage-powered lightning bolts, and even spine chilling blizzards (a feat that even impressed Virella Frost). Like all shade mages, Thyra Volt derives her powers from the negative aspects of magic.

Thyra Volt's spells all take the forms of tornadoes, storm clouds and lightning bolts; all of which cause large amounts of damage, no matter how minor the spell is. Thyra Volt is even capable of manipulating other objects through various ways, such as by using the strong winds of her tornadoes to pick things up and throw them about, or by using electrical shocks to engage in not so subtle body control and machine malfunctions. The Aurora Circle Comic Series also suggests that Thyra Volt possesses the ability to manipulate gaseous substances as she can be seen generating helium to blow up a hot-air balloon in Issue 20, and emitting a sleeping gas on the pixies in Issue 26.

By Cycle 3, Thyra Volt showcases the ability to create much stronger storms with more control over their direction through the use of a magical fan. She has shown the ability to create various natural disasters such as a small tidal wave capable of flooding pixie village and creating a storm clouds to transform into her shade mage outfit. She is also given the ability to create Storm Harpies: half-women, half-bird creatures that can also create their own wind gusts. Thyra Volt herself can also be seen generating limited earthquakes that are usually the results of her severe storms; something she shares in common with Selka Shade.

Throughout the seasons, Thyra Volt's powers grow stronger, which is made evident by her more focused and potent lightning bolts, some of which she can even use to tie up her opponents along with creating an electrical force field. She also showcases the ability to create multiple tornadoes at once with ease as seen with her Double Tornado spell; a comparable improvement to when she needed absolute focus to create two of them at the same time in the episode "Lyra Vale's Dark Secret." Her powers over electricity are enhanced in the Cycle 6 episode "A Monster Crush" when she enters the Myth Archive World to assume the role of the Bride of Frankenstein's Monster, and the rest of her powers receive a significant boost following Thyra Volt's merge with Occula upon absorbing a large quantity of Wild Magic in Cycle 7.

As a senior shade mage at Noctis Spire during her Cycle 1 debut, it is safe to assume that Thyra Volt may know some basic, first-level spells that all shade mages can use, such as telekinesis, transmuting objects, fixing minor messes and flying. Her magic is often seen as being bright purple to even red in color, and when getting a power boost from Tritannus (and Politea) she was seen with a toxic/neon green aura.
