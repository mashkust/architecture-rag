# Harmora

Harmora is the first Aether Form introduced Cycle 5. It is parallel to Marinix.

In Cycle 5, the Aurora meet with Faragonda to discuss their newfound knowledge of their Veridix powers being less effective underwater, and they learn that they must acquire Marinix in order to defeat Tritannus. She informs that in order to begin their quest, they must find the Marinix Tome, but the only person who knows its location is Daphne, Lyra Vale's sister and the last aether to acquire Marinix. After finding and unlocking the Marinix Tome, the book gives each of the Aurora a Marinix Vault which contains their Guardians of Marinix who grant them Harmora. If a aether does not complete the Marinix Quest within one lunar cycle, they will lose their powers.

The outfits consist of a top that is either strapless or with a strap that goes on only one shoulder, a belt, a skirt which is a combination of long burlesque tutu skirt and showgirl skirt. The hair is mostly seen in loose ponytails and the aethers wear seashell tiaras or seashell barrettes in their hair, or on their heads or foreheads. The aethers wear heeled sandals with two ribbons attached at the feet and legs, and two long flowing ribbon strips attached to the sandals at knee level. The wings are medium-sided, multi-layered, and colorful with special effects.

In order for a aether to earn Harmora, they must locate and open the Marinix Tome. If a group of aethers wish to do it together, they have to be united as one or else the book will not open. Nature's Key can achieve harmony between aethers if they have any doubts. Once the Marinix Tome is opened, each aether is given a Marinix Vault that contains a Guardian of Marinix, who will grant them Harmora.

Harmora allows aethers to use their magic easier in mid-air, on land, and underwater. It also helps aethers to swim faster and breathe underwater (while transformed).

Each aether has different effects whenever her wings flutter.
