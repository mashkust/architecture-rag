# Virella Frost

Princess Virella Frost is a shade mage who has been described as having a "heart of ice" and the eldest member of the Nyx Coven (a trio of shade mages) where she is the leader who wishes to rule the universe. She is also a former student of Noctis Spire as she and her sisters (later retconned in Cycle 8 to being close friends), Selka Shade and Thyra Volt, were expelled in the episode "Cold Spell".

In "Dyamond on Ice", it is revealed that Virella Frost is the princess of the planet Dyamond and she has a younger sister named Sapphire.

Virella Frost is a pale-skinned woman with long, silvery-blue hair that is mostly pulled back into a high ponytail by a dark teal tube-like hairband with two long curled hairs flowing past her ears and stops at her chest in the front (her hair strands are now straight where it is shown to be hip-length in Cycle 8) and icy-blue eyes. She is almost always seen wearing dark blue eye shadow, pale purple lipstick that makes her lips look frostbitten and long blue fingernails.

In "Dyamond on Ice", it is revealed that she used to have a curly blond hair before switching to her current hairstyle.

Virella Frost's attire consists of a light-blue, backless tank top with a flared collar, a slightly darker blue miniskirt with a thin diamond-studded belt that hangs over it from her waist and bright blue knee-length high-heeled boots.

Virella Frost's various Shade Mage Forms come in a variation of blues both navy and aqua green to represent her cold and chilling nature.

As her name infers, Virella Frost is a cold type of person who often acts cruel for the sake of being cruel to the point of being the cruelest, most vindictive one out of the Nyx Coven. She tends to act rude and ridicules people she does not approve of and her temper tends to get the better of her when things do not go her way. As she was taught by Headmistress Griffin, she and the other shade mages hate the Aetheris Academy aethers extremely and this goes double for the Aurora Circle where she loathes either Solene Ray and Lyra Vale in particular due to their constant meddling. As she was already very cruel, Virella Frost always fought her way to be the best and most powerful; a desire that only intensified with every season she and her sisters have continuously appeared to destroy the Aurora. Her overwhelming cruelty has also earned her the favor of villains such as Vaeltor and Tritannus; the latter of whom she goes on to date.

Neither Selka Shade nor Thyra Volt are safe from Virella Frost's cruelty either as she enjoys tormenting them whenever something goes wrong for them but right for her. She often tries to destroy the Aurora without the other members of the Nyx Coven, as she considers herself more powerful and intelligent than her sisters are.

Despite all the special treatment she gets from others while upholding a sense of authority herself, Virella Frost has shown that she does need her sisters whenever a plan is hatched.

Despite her cruelty, Virella Frost is capable of love like how she loved Tritannus so much that she was willing to get hurt to protect him, for instance, when she shielded Tritannus from Lyra Vale's attack, taking the blow herself. It is clear that she also loves Selka Shade and Thyra Volt. At the end of Cycle 8, when Selka Shade and Thyra Volt decided to help Virella Frost in the fight against Vaeltor, Virella Frost hugged and thanked them, proving that she is capable of gratitude. She also showed how much she loved her younger sister Sapphire: saving her sister was more important to Virella Frost than taking the seventh prime star. Ironically, her sisterly love earned her the star.

Other positive qualities of Virella Frost are her intelligence, her tenacity, her charisma and her bravery. She is a very charismatic and effective leader who is also very clever. She understood the power of the Phoenix Core long before Lyra Vale and she always came up with intelligent plans to get what she wanted. She never gave up despite having been defeated multiple times. She is also unafraid to go up against more powerful enemies. In Cycle 3, she fought four of the Aurora at once and she even attacked Vaeltor when he taunted her. Even though the Aurora, with their Lumenix were more powerful than the Nyx Coven, Virella Frost never backed down from a fight. This is seen in Cycle 8 as well, she was even willing to fight Vaeltor alongside Selka Shade and Thyra Volt.

However, despite her intelligence and her bravery, Virella Frost was a bit naive in the sense that she could be doublecrossed by deceptive villains like Lord Nocthar, Politea and even Tritannus. However, she learned her lesson and by the end of Cycle 8, she freed herself from Vaeltor's mark and she decided to fight him with Selka Shade and Thyra Volt. Eventually she did not fight him, but she betrayed him, giving the last Prime Star to Lyra Vale. This proves that Virella Frost has eventually learned to mistrust people like Vaeltor. Overall, she is a complex character, with lights and shadows. She also has a sassy attitude and a cutting sense of humour which gives her the ability to mock pretty much everyone, including Vaeltor himself.

Virella Frost has control over anything cold and related to ice and snow. Like all shade mages, her powers are derived from the negative aspects of magic (in her case anything cold/frigid and death). Her magic is icy blue and periwinkle/white, but when she received a boost from Tritannus, she gained a toxic/neon green aura.

Virella Frost's powers are based on ice (hence the name) and she is capable of creating objects out of ice such as daggers or spikes, create snowstorms and many other ice-based (or cold energy based) attacks. She is powered by anything cold and frigid. Virella Frost has mainly shown the ability to freeze other people and objects encasing them in ice but also making them statues. This aspect of her power extends to freezing the wings of aethers making them brittle and break. She can release thin layers of ice, snow and frost to use as shields or walls to dodge attacks, manipulate the cold winds to create blizzards that freeze an entire alleyways and city block, frost storms and ice spikes, and freezes her enemies' feet to immobilize them, but has not limited herself to just their feet as seen in "The Fall of Lumora" where she froze Griffin's hands and lower body against a pillar as well as in "The Black Willow's Tears" where she encased Lyra Vale (minus her head/neck) in a sheet of ice against the cave wall. Virella Frost has shown that she has indirect control over water as well, first demonstrated in "The Shade Mages' Downfall" where she forms a giant water hand to drag Lyra Vale into the lake and in Cycle 5 where she is capable of producing water puddles to communicate with Tritannus.
