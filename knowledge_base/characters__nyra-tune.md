# Nyra Tune

Nyra Tune is the Aether of Harmony, but prefers to be known as the Aether of Music. She hails from Sonara and currently acts as its Guardian Aether. Nyra Tune is the daughter of Matlin and Ho-Boe, and is the girlfriend of Riven Thorne. She and her boyfriend, Riven Thorne, have a rocky and difficult but passionate relationship. Nyra Tune is a skilled singer and musician. She is one of the founding members of the Aurora Circle, being the fifth Aurora girl introduced after Lyra Vale, Solene Ray, Eira Thorn and Vexa Quill, and is an alumna/student of the Aetheris Academy College for Aethers.

Nyra Tune has a light, almost pale skin tone, dark blue hair and thin dark blue eyes. While initially kept in short pigtails, Nyra Tune has let her hair grow out from Cycle 3 onwards thanks to a spell that lengthened it down to her waist, and has since styled it in many other ways, especially in comparison to the other Aurora girls.

For both seasons her outfit consists of an ocean blue top with a variation of blue and pink gemstones and a pink bow decal along with a teal tank underneath. Her skirt is a mixture of pink and blue with cutouts shaping triangles and diamonds along with a detailed belt with blue and white rhinestones. Her stockings are different variations and textures of blue with pink high heels. On her left arm she has a studded aqua bracelet while on her right she has a watch with a few hooped bracelets. Nyra Tune's hair is a sapphire blue with bangs in the front with lengthy waves in the back.

Though she is recognized as the Aether of Music, anything related to music acts as a minor detail within most of Nyra Tune's Aether Forms, like the headphones she wears in her Aurora, the note-shaped wings she gains in Veridix, or just any small note-shaped accessories.

Interestingly, her color palette also makes a major shift; starting out from red with her Aurora and gaining yellows and golds as accents with Lumenix, only for blue to replace the yellows in Veridix and onward. The hard red that initially acted as the primary color to her Aether Forms gradually became more and more pink, even shifting to purple completely with Florenix.

Nyra Tune loves music, dancing, singing, and playing all instruments, but her favorite instrument is the concert flute, the first instrument she ever learned to play. Nyra Tune usually plays amazing music, but plays best when she is alone by herself in Aetheris Academy in a quiet spot. Despite being the most tomboyish of the Aurora, she is perhaps the most emotionally vulnerable, and she balances this by putting up a tough front. Surprisingly (considering Vexa Quill is the brain of the group), Nyra Tune gets the best grades of her friends, as mentioned in "Honor Above All". Her emotional weakness is the result of her mother's death and her father is far away. She is also a bit of a loner though over time she becomes more open and closely bonded to the rest of her friends. She also loses more of her tomboyish attitudes and appearance over time.

She has a strong will and personality which unfortunately makes her clash with her boyfriend Riven Thorne the most among the group, which even cumulates in their break-up in Cycle 6. Nyra Tune shows her maturity by that choice, understanding that relationships do not always work out, and it is perfectly fine to go separate paths, yet without animosity towards each other. She can, however, be judgemental and have preconceived notions about people regarding their past, which can make her pretty distrusting, as demonstrated by her attitude towards Riven Thorne in Cycle 8.

Nyra Tune, as the Aether of Music, has music-based powers. Nyra Tune's powers are connected not just with music but the actual power of sound waves and sonic pressure. Nyra Tune can manipulate things by creating sonic booms which cause rumbling and crashes. She can also create sonic vibes and pulses which charge through the air like energy blasts. Nyra Tune is able to create sonic waves which hypnotize and block the line of sight. Nyra Tune can unleash disrupting sonic booms which can split the earth and crash in the air. She can create disco-ball force-fields, distracting musical blasts, and virtual speakers which enclose around an enemy and immobilize them through incredibly loud bass music. Nyra Tune can also create walls and barriers of the Grand Staff.

Nyra Tune is also able to release sound waves that can be used to calm others and even put them to sleep. Nyra Tune can release mental sound waves to communicate with other civilians from Sonara and displays this again with her aether animal. Nyra Tune has developed the ability to control others (bodies) by dancing, generating a miniature earthquake by placing her hand on the ground and generating low-pressure sound waves, and even imprisoning others in a sound field. She is capable of controlling musical instruments allowing her to silence, conjure, and levitate them. Nyra Tune is capable of casting spells by playing her flute channeling her magical music into barriers, hypnotizing minds, and summoning animals. By Cycle 6 her singing voice alone is capable of enchanting objects namely musical instruments as seen with the Music Cafe.

Nyra Tune also knows basic first-level spells such as telekinesis, transmuting objects, fixing minor messes etc. Naturally, as the Guardian aether of music; Nyra Tune is a naturally gifted singer even getting a record deal from a famous music producer. This also applies to musical instruments as she is able to recognize the sound of any musical instrument as well as playing them with ease. Nyra Tune learned to opera sing from her mother allowing her to communicate with birds. She has naturally enhanced hearing along with a strong willed voice.

When Nyra Tune and Eira Thorn were fighting Vaeltor, he was able to weaken her with the power of silence meaning that she is weakened if there is no sound. Not only is she weaken when their is no sound but was not even able to transform and only use basic spells when her voice was stolen in "The Music Cafe".

She is also considered somewhat of a telepathic aether, which was showcased in the comics as she was able to reveal the memories and feelings of someone through song.

This time around, Nyra Tune uses her powers in more strategic and practical ways than she does either offensively or defensively. She is capable of picking up on a person or creature's location by sending out sound waves like a radar in a similar way to echolocation and does so in many instances like when she needed to locate Annabelle and the Crocodile Man in the episodes "New Powers" and "The Fall of the Queen" respectively. By Cycle 2, this ability of hers is further amplified upon gaining Onyrix as her special Onyrix Power, Sound Harmony, allows her to hone in on a target's sounds so that she can effectively track them down. She can also keep a person's vocal imprint memorized in case she needs to track them down at a later time. She is also capable of recreating certain sounds to lure enemies out of hiding and this can be seen when she lures the Crocodile Man out by replicating the sound of footsteps in the episode "The Legend of the Crocodile Man."
