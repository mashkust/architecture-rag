# Aetheris Academy

The Aetheris Academy College for Aethers is a girls' boarding school in Lumora for Aethers and the most prestigious educational institution for aethers.

Aetheris Academy is an ancient school for aethers, and apparently the only one dedicated to aether education only. The Aetheris Academy students are mostly in training ages 16-19 (unless they have been held back a grade like Solene Ray, so in her case, 17). Aethers here learn how to control their power, fight evil, learn new spells as well as be guardians of their planets, or in many cases, learn how to be their realms' queens. In Cycle 4, it is here that the Aurora, after graduating, taught for a short period of time.

“The aether students come to college here to learn how to cast spells, create enchanted auras and make magical potions! Potionology, metamorphosimbiosis, magiphilosophy and good manners... are among the complex subjects taught in the college by very skilled, strict and demanding teachers! Thanks to these lessons, the aspiring teenage aethers learn how to use and improve their powers in order to fight against evil in the name of equality, brotherhood and justice! The Aetheris Academy students live within the college itself, which has an enormous, well stocked library, classrooms, and a Magical Archive where the most important secrets of Lumora are kept!”

Additional classes include dreams (second year), cognitive analysis (which is a subject of technical magic), applied convergence, magic invocation, magic self-defense, Auroralogy, biotransformation, magiphysics, History of Magic, Cosmomagic, and Botany Magics. The education in Aetheris Academy lasts three years, one for each Aether Form in the journey of an aspiring full-fledged aether.

When Mavilla was headmistress of Aetheris Academy, owning and exhibiting the strengths of one's Aether Animal was considered a vital part of a aether education. Everything taught was a reflection of the contents of the Tome of Nature. Eventually, Mavilla realized Aetheris Academy was like a big cage to Aether Animals and the current curriculum prohibited them from living freely. At another point in time, Eldora was the keeper of the Aetheris Academy Greenhouse and taught floral magic.

Freshmen aethers learn how to master their own source of power and learn many basic spells they can use in their daily life as well as spells cast spontaneously and on instinct. Learning how to change one's appearance in metamorphosis class and making potions in Potionology are part of this year's education. Aethers also learn how to explore diverse environments or complete certain tasks in the Simulation Room. Choosing one of the two prompts is a aether's midterm. Some training exercises are also required.

In their cognitive analysis class, aethers increase their magical abilities by learning technical incantations, and spells requiring cognitive analysis and accurate pronunciation. Other classes include magic invocation, which teaches the practice of making magic through technical charms, magic self-defense, which teaches how to defend oneself through the use of spells, and applied convergence, which teaches how to effectively combine powers. They also have the goal of earning Charmara, a power-up that will temporarily boost their energy. After earning it, the intensity of their training exercises is increased.

After spring break, aethers are tasked with their final exam; earning Lumenix, the Final Aether Form in a aether's formal educational journey to become a full-fledged aether. Those who achieve Lumenix will participate in training exercises to hone their abilities.

After earning Lumenix, the basic aether education is completed and they will receive the titles of Guardian Aethers of their respective homeworlds on the Day of the Gift.

It is worth noting that even after graduating, the Aurora returned to normal school life at Aetheris Academy starting in Cycle 5. The behind-the-scenes reason for this is a reboot of the Aurora' age to prolong the series.

Its campus is a large castle with pink walls and blue roofs built around a courtyard itself having a well that leads to underground tunnels linking the three schools of Lumora together.

The classrooms, school hospital, kitchen, and school hall are mostly located on ground level, while the rooms of the staff members and the dormitories of the students are on the first floor. On the first floor, there is also the library, the office of the headmistress, and the school archives, found in one of the towers of the castle and guarded by the Glimmer Concorda.

Protective barriers meant to keep non-magical creatures and bad weather out are seen many times in the series. Their power source is known to be aether magic or Aether Animal magic.
