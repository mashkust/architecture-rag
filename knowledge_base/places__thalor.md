# Thalor

Thalor is the realm of oceans and is thus connected to all the oceans of other realms within the Aether Realm. It is also the home of mermaids and tritons.

Thalor is a planet predominantly covered in water (90% according to the official website) with a few jungle islands that include the island that houses the Omega Portal, Black Island and the Islands of Nadur, Nantros and Guanaco. Many of these islands seem to be volcanic as Black Island, as its name would entail, is covered by volcanic ash, and the Island of Nantros contains a volcano itself. Pirates also encircle these islands, making a few of them into their bases to operate illegally while evading arrest from Thalor' royal guards. Abandoned ruins of ancient buildings also rest both above and below the ocean waves which were never explained.

Thalor' society is divided into two main ones: a surface society of the inhabitants of dry land and an underwater society of the inhabitants of the sea. Both societies are run by their own monarchies that work in tandem with one another. Both worlds have a good and respected monarchy and good authority.

The Aurora Circle Comic Series also reveals that Thalor has problems with piracy in many parts of the realm. They have difficulty apprehending the pirates involved, especially since they can never uncover the locations of their hideouts and trade ports.

The inhabitants of dry land include aethers, humans, and arcane keepers. The kingdom is currently led by King Teredor and Queen Niobe, with their only daughter, Princess Mira Tide as the heir to the throne. Many nobles also reside in the surface kingdom, one of which include Nabir Ashen's family, which is regarded as the richest family on Thalor. As Mira Tide mentioned in her first appearance, the royal family of Thalor has always been friendly with the Glimmers and visits them at Glimmer Hollow every year.

The society of the surface kingdom appears to be more conservative and traditional in comparison to most other realms. This can be gleaned from Solene Ray's claims in Cycle 3, as Mira Tide responds by claiming that the process of a realm's king and queen publicly announcing a princess' upcoming wedding to a groom that they had chosen and approved of still goes on in some realms; hinting at the fact that the royals of Thalor still engage in the tradition. This is later proven in the episode "The Heart and the Sword" where Teredor, Niobe and Nabir Ashen's parents arrange a wedding between Mira Tide and Nabir Ashen without either of their consent.

The inhabitants of the undersea kingdom include the sexually dimorphic merfolk: the females being the mermaids and the males being the tritons. They are currently led by Triton King Neptune and Mermaid Queen Ligea, with their son Crown Prince Nereus as heir to the throne respectively. Neptune is also the brother of Teredor, the king of the terrestrial kingdom.

Tritannus, the younger twin brother of Nereus, was removed from his position in the royal family upon being disowned by Neptune for his attempts at dethroning his family to seize the power of the oceans for himself by attempting to kill his own brother. Nereus was crowned as heir to the triton throne, which acted as the inciting incident for Tritannus to attack during the ceremony. Even if Tritannus had done nothing, he would have remained a prince as Nereus became King of the Oceans.

While King Neptune commands an army of tritons, it is the warrior mermaids who are tasked with guarding the Omega Portal. Mermaids also served as royal guards to King Neptune and his family as they attended the Council of Sovereigns being held on Elyndor. The merfolk have wings (mermaids) or fins (tritons) that allow them to fly, as well as leave the water for some time.

The existence of magical links between Thalor and the oceans of all other planets in the Aether Realm is made as of Cycle 3 when Mira Tide had to swim through the oceans of Lumora to return to Thalor and answer a distress call. According to both Mira Tide and Tressa, the depth of Thalor' oceans does not have a definitive bottom, and Mira Tide even referred to the Oceans of Thalor as the "Heart of All Oceans" when a dimensional shock wave triggered a tidal wave on Lumora after Vaeltor force opened the Omega Portal. These magical links later become known as Ocean Gates by Cycle 5; each of which is guarded by a specialized Selkie.

In Perle, it expound many things regarding the terrestrial and undersea kingdoms.

In the past, one of the kings of Thalor was Eidonte, with Ligea as heir instead of her half-sister Vires, which resulted in Thalor' Obscure Force prophecy that talked about the throne "usurped from birth" and Thalor no longer being if no sacrifice was made. When Ligea and Vires crashes in war, Ligea's sacrifices herself to save her daughter and sister, breaking the prophecy, saving Thalor and freeing the mermaids.

In Magic Ocean, the Acquarioteca was called as the secret library of the Kingdom of the Sea, said to house millions of volumes, and contained the Fortune Teller Book (Thalor Enemies book).
