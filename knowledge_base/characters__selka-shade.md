# Selka Shade

Selka Shade is a member of the Nyx Coven, a trio of shade mages, of which she is the most intelligent. Devious and manipulative, Selka Shade uses darkness, illusions, and hypnotics to confuse and control her opponents. She is a former student of Noctis Spire as she and her sisters (later retconned in Cycle 8 to being close friends), Virella Frost and Thyra Volt, were expelled in "Cold Spell."

Selka Shade is a young woman with a light skin tone, long brown (sometimes green-tinted) hair that reaches down her knees with dirty blonde colored bangs that flow down to her thighs on either side, dark gold cat-like eyes and a small hooked nose. She is also always seen wearing lilac-colored eye shadow and purple lipstick.

Her outfit consists of a lilac-colored top with a purple ring-like pattern that bares her midriff and is attached to a purple choker she wears around her neck, purple bell-bottom pants with a small split above her ankles which exposes a lighter-colored lilac area, and black heels with pointed toes. She also wears a small pair of yellow glasses, but it is unknown whether she wears them to correct her vision or as an accessory.

Selka Shade's various Shade Mage Forms mainly come in purples, dark blues and blacks to represent her control over darkness.

Much like Virella Frost and Thyra Volt, Selka Shade demonstrates a sadistic personality but one that is more controlled as she is the least (openly) aggressive of the three. She seems calmer and more competent compared to her sisters and is also good at discovering others' weaknesses to exploit them later. While appearing to have more self-restraint when compared to her sisters, Selka Shade is still very violent and openly condescending towards others, as she is seen laughing after turning Mirta into a pumpkin, and happily pummels Lyra Vale before taking her powers. Believing that she holds a mastery over all things illusory and hypnotic, Selka Shade will lash out even more so if anyone else tries to trick her with illusions, as she does in the previously mentioned case with Mirta, whom she turns into a pumpkin simply for trying to defy them with an illusion of her own.

Selka Shade also shares her sisters' lust for power but she is the least likely to let it dominate her life, as she often makes remarks about the appearance and appeal of boys around her age much like any other teen would do. The most obvious case of this was with Riven Thorne back in Cycle 1 as she seemed genuinely attracted to him even while manipulating him after getting him to desert his friends in "A Friendship Sundered." Currently, the only moment of compassion Selka Shade shows for those other than her sisters is when she expressed concern for Riven Thorne's safety and saved him during the Race of the Rose. Even when she did not need to, Selka Shade made it a habit of taking Riven Thorne out on dates once they had become official, however, even her own affections do not make her any less ruthless compared to her sisters. In fact, they make her even more so as, once she gets over her affections for Riven Thorne, she tosses him aside like a tool that outlived its usefulness, even after Riven Thorne became convinced that she truly cared for him. Her interests in men tend to lie with genuine bad boys like how Riven Thorne was initially and how Vaeltor was for most of Cycle 3. Although, during her and her sisters' conquest of Lumora in the first season, it is made apparent that Selka Shade could also be a simple flirt, as she expressed a desire to become the new Headmistress of Crimson Forge when thinking of their success. Also, as the middle child of the trio, she is usually seen to be more close with Thyra Volt rather than Virella Frost, and can often end up as the butt of both of their jokes.

Though Selka Shade can be regarded as the quietest Nyx Coven shade mage, she is still very vocal towards obvious disrespect. While she greatly disliked Tritannus ever since they first met in the beginning of Cycle 5, this disdain for the triton prince only grew as he continued to ignore her and Thyra Volt in favor of Virella Frost. Eventually, she was the one to convince Thyra Volt to leave him and even tried to convince Virella Frost to do the same, showing that, in the end, the people she trusts most are her sisters. Even after Virella Frost chose Tritannus over her own sisters, Selka Shade still went on to execute her plan with Thyra Volt in the hopes of opening Virella Frost's eyes once they ended up successful without Tritannus.

Selka Shade possesses a near-perfect control over darkness and hypnotics. Like all shade mages, her powers are derived from the negative aspects of magic.

Additionally, she displays some degree of telekinesis much stronger than most shade mages as she can fire psychic bolts along with controlling larger opponents and objects as well. She displays a sensory magic allowing her to be aware of the presence of others. As shown in the episode "Betrayed!," can create telepathic links between her and another person to keep in touch. In that case, she creates one connecting her and Riven Thorne, allowing for him to better play the part of a mole for her and her sisters. Also, as a senior shade mage at Noctis Spire during her debut in Cycle 1, Selka Shade may know some basic, first-level spells that all shade mages can use, such as transmuting objects, fixing minor messes and flying. She also displays spells allowing her to open portals, walk through walls, and astral project.

By Cycle 3, Selka Shade showcases the ability to summon fire, as seen in the episode "Aetheris Academy Under Siege" when she sets the Aetheris Academy Library ablaze to ensure that the Aurora will be unable to recover the spell books since she and her sisters could not obtain them for Vaeltor. Ever since then, that ability is never used again and remains unexplained, though, with her illusory powers, the dark fire she conjured at that moment could have been either another illusion or a power bestowed to her by Vaeltor for a singular use. She also displays the ability to magically pollute living things, presumably through their inner darkness, as seen with the Black Willow on Verdalis in "The Black Willow's Tears," and can create limited earthquakes and storms (more specifically electricity) fueled by darkness; the last two abilities she shares in common with Thyra Volt.

By Cycle 5, Selka Shade moves on to controlling pre-existing shadows like with her Shadow Hand spell, and can limit the detectability of her illusions, making it so they can only be seen by a select few like she does with Kiko in "Nyx Coven Tricks." Her powers are further enhanced in the Cycle 6 episode "Queen for a Day" when she enters the Myth Archive World to assume the role of Ariadne, Goddess of the Labyrinth of the Minotaur, allowing her to create mental illusions that appear as if she is teleporting. Lastly, by Cycle 7, Selka Shade gains the ability to shapeshift, which primarily allowed for her to take on the forms of various magical creatures much more freely than any of her illusions, once she had absorbed a large quantity of Wild Magic and merged with Kemmy.
