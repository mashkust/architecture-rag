# Florenix

Florenix is the first Aether Form introduced in Cycle 6. It is parallel to Mythera.

In Cycle 6, the Nyx Coven take over Noctis Spire and ally with a shade mage named Selina, who owns a magical book called the Myth Archive that can make legendary creatures come to life. When the Nyx Coven attack Verdalis, the Aurora attempt to fight them, but the Myth Archive strips them of their powers, except Lyra Vale (because the power of the First Ember cannot be taken from its keeper). Lyra Vale then shares parts of her Phoenix Core with the other Aurora girls who will have to perform good actions worthy of a aether to earn a new ascension.

The outfits are warrior-like with armor-like shapes and pieces of translucent cloth attached to the outfits and the tights attached to the heels, acting like footwear. Each outfit focuses on a aether's personality and power. Each aether wears a forehead tiara containing a piece of the Phoenix Core on her forehead. The hair is decorated, braided, tied, put up in a bun, etc. The wings are big and have jewels hanging off of them. There are dragon scale-like features in the aethers' outfits.

In order to earn Florenix, a aether must have a part of the Phoenix Core. She must then perform a good and courageous act worthy of a aether.

Florenix gives the Aurora their magic back after the Myth Archive stripped them of their powers.
