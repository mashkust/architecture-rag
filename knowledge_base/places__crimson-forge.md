# Crimson Forge

The Crimson Forge School for Wardens (Crimson Forge School for Magicians in Cycle 1) is one of the three main schools of Lumora, along with Aetheris Academy and Noctis Spire, and is where Wardens are trained.

Crimson Forge is a paramilitary boarding school for Wardens, and the most prestigious educational institution for Wardens. It is located in Lumora across Lake Roccaluce from both Noctis Spire and Aetheris Academy and appears to be a very prestigious school.

Crimson Forge is charged with the defense of the Realm of Lumora and it was called the "Fortress of the Aether Realm" by Codatorta.

In the first season of Aurora Circle, it was also said to be a school for Arcane Keepers but the only residents of Crimson Forge who have ever been seen using magic are the Headmaster Saladin, and Athena, the Guardian Glimmer of the school's fragment of the Aether Key. However, in "Secrets Within Secrets", it was seen that the Wardens could control the dragons of Crimson Forge through a magic or telepathic bond.

“The students can become true warriors here, skilled in the use of magic and technology! Crimson Forge is the Lumora college that trains the bravest boys in the Magical Dimension to become qualified Lumora experts. They help the aethers and are always ready to lend a hand. The students live in the college. They learn how to use all kinds of weapons, from the most traditional to the most technological. They drive special vehicles that they can use on their missions. Each one has his own personal dragon that he trains with. Crimson Forge was destroyed by the wicked Nyx Coven but rose again from the ashes. Now [it is] even more imposing.”

Crimson Forge trains young men to fight using normal and magical methods, in hopes to serve their own home world's military one day. They practice wu-gong, the same type of complex martial arts practiced by the templars of the Fortress of Light, but at a less developed level.

Students are trained with classical weapons like swords and maces and modern weapons like blasters. The students also learn to ride dragons.

The school has a diverse array of classes from basic survival and dragon wrangling, to close quarter combat and even flying fighter crafts, and wilderness training.

The school has also been noted to teach magic, but no student has ever been seen using magic.

Additional classes include forest orientation and advanced rescue.

Students wear a mandatory uniform, consisting of a blue and yellow body suit and a blue cape. The cape attaches to a round jewel worn on the left breast.

The choice of weapons are usually dependent on preference and skill set, but mainly consist of swords/phantoblades. Students also have a choice of what sword they use. They have a choice of broadswords, long swords, rapiers, halberds and scimitars (although there are probably more not seen in the series).

All Crimson Forge weapons are made of absolute magic-proof material (although the wielder may suffer from knock back force should a powerful spell hit the weapon) and are retractable into the hilt.

Some exotic weapons for personal preference have also been seen (claws or just a pair of gloves for unarmed combat are preferred by some Specialist seen in Cycle 1). Bows with glowing arrows were used in the defense of Aetheris Academy against the Army of Darkness.

Other devices include a short-range teleporter and a levitating shield-shaped hoverboard used for quick travel.
