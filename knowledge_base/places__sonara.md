# Sonara

Sonara is the realm of music, for which many of the inhabitants are naturally inclined toward more than other people in the entire Aether Realm. It is the homeworld of Nyra Tune and Princess Galatea.

The realm was initially seen only in flashbacks, Nyra Tune's memories, or simply mentioned. Its princess, Galatea, attended Aetheris Academy and became fast friends with Nyra Tune. She was saved by her from a fire the Nyx Coven started in the Aetheris Academy library, which earned Nyra Tune her Lumenix.

Like the majority of the Aether Realm, the planet is ruled by monarchy. The King of Sonara appears to be a heavyset man and his clothing is similar to the traditional dress of Earth's Emperor of China, however his appearance differs in the comics. He is the father of Princess Galatea. He and his daughter participated in the Council of Sovereigns, a summit that was held at the Royal Palace of Elyndor during the events of Cycle 5.

The Queen of Sonara appears in Issue 144: The Enchanted Sonara during the Festival of Harmony on Sonara.

Sonara is full of mountains that are surrounded by forests with reddish leaf trees.

There is an ocean near Nyra Tune's home, in which there exists the Ocean Gate linking Sonara to Thalor that is guarded by a Selkie named Sonna, as well as the Ruby Reef, which is filled with beautiful corals and a giant enchanted harp created from those corals that allow it to make beautiful music upon being played.

Sonara debuts in "Cold Spell" when the Aurora were having nightmares. While sleeping, Nyra Tune walks around Sonara and the lake from season 5 can seen. A Chinese style song is playing. Nyra Tune sees her mother and then she disappears.

Sonara appears in "The Show Must Go On!", where Nyra Tune is seen visiting her mother's grave in the realm. She talks to her mother about her doubts, fears, concerns, sadness, and the conflict between her and her father Ho-Boe, who disagrees with Nyra Tune's dream to becoming a singer due to fear of potentially losing her like he lost his wife.

The two would eventually reach an understanding and they both visit Matlin's together, sharing a warm hug and looking at the sky, fondly remembering her.

Sonara is mentioned by Galatea, introducing herself as the Princess of Sonara. It is then mentioned again during Vaeltor and the Nyx Coven's attack on Aetheris Academy, where Galatea is captured by the Nyx Coven and communicates with Nyra Tune through low frequency mental waves, in which the latter informs the Aurora that this is method of emergency communication used on Sonara.

Sonara appears in "Secret of the Ruby Reef", where the Aurora solve part of a riddle for the location of the Gem of Empathy. It reminds Nyra Tune of her past and time she spent with her mother. After she, Solene Ray and Mira Tide used their Harmora, Tritannus - who had forced himself through the Ocean Gate by absorbing the powers of Sonna, appeared and tried to turn Mira Tide into an monster, but Nyra Tune jumped in front of the ray and was turned into a monster instead.

Being chased by Nyra Tune through the ocean, Solene Ray and Mira Tide hid in an cave and Lemmy got an idea of making Nyra Tune bond with Sonna to restore her to her original form. While Solene Ray distracted Nyra Tune, Mira Tide got Sonna along with Lemmy.

Sonna and Nyra Tune met rather dramatically and suddenly but Nyra Tune got her original form back and Sonna got her powers back. After this, they traveled nearby to the Ruby Reef and Nyra Tune found the coral harp and played her mother's song.

Sonara appears again in "The Singing Whales". After Nyra Tune told Riven Thorne how much she missed her mother and his sudden need to leave, Nyra Tune reveals that it is the Singing Whales Day and calls her father. Ho-Boe answers and he shows Nyra Tune the whales.

After Tritannus successfully takes the seal from the Pillar of Balance, imbalance began to displace itself amongst the magic dimension. Feeling abandoned from Virella Frost and Tritannus, Selka Shade and Thyra Volt stage their own evil deed - kidnapping the singing whales of Sonara.

When the Aurora enter the Endless Deep, the singing whales appear, bewitched by Selka Shade and Thyra Volt, and are ordered to finish them off. As the Aurora get ready to defend themselves against them, Nyra Tune reveals that the whales are vital to Sonara's survival. Nyra Tune then sings her mother's song and relinquishes them from the Nyx Coven's spell and the whales return to Sonara.

The Aurora return later to Sonara and Nyra Tune joins her father and pines for her mother, thinking of using her Marinix wish to bring her back but decides not to. Nyra Tune and her friends then return home and the whales wave goodbye to her.

When the Aurora and Daphne receive an invitation to the Golden Auditorium from Diletta, Selina summons the Pandemonium Sprites to attack them. However, Nyra Tune and Vexa Quill found a way to defeat them and earned their Florenix.

In Aether Insider, it says that "Ludwig van Boothaven" is a famous composer from Sonara that composed the Number Five and Half symphony.
