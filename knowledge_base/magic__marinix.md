# Marinix

Marinix is the ancient, original Aether Form that was created from the magic of the oceans, and it is considered to be the the supreme power in all the oceans. It is parallel but superior to Harmora.

The Marinix was an ancient power that was originally created from the magic of the primordial oceans in antiquity, presumably around the time of the formation of the Endless Deep, and it was the oldest known power in the Aether Realm. It is also the greatest power in all the oceans, of which little is known and it could not be easily found in any known magical databases. Its powers had been sought after before the time of Daphne and the Aurora by many aethers and nymphs across the centuries, including Politea, though there were known some failures.

The Source of Marinix is found in the cave where Daphne dwells in Lake Roccaluce which protects Marinix and grants it to aethers who have completed the Marinix Quest.

Prior to the Aurora, Daphne was the last aether to acquire the ancient power of the oceans. After acquiring their Marinix, Daphne and Politea confronted the three Primeval Shadows in the Endless Deep, where the trio cast a terrible curse on Daphne's Marinix, that would turn her into a disembodied spiritual being should she try to use her new Marinix powers.

Politea, who refused to help her friend in her most desperate time, was also cursed by the Shade Mages and turned into a ravenous sea-beast once her powers were fully corrupted. With the last two Marinix aethers defeated, the Shade Mages would go on to curse Marinix itself to ensure that no other aether would think of achieving it.

Aware that the Dragon's Flame had been passed down to Oritel and Marion's baby girl, the Primeval Shadows sought Lyra Vale out as they left Vaeltor to do battle with the Company alone during their raid on Elyndor. As they raided the castle, they chased Daphne down for Lyra Vale but, just as the Dragon's Flame was within their reach, Daphne sent Lyra Vale to a place where the Shade Mages would never be able to sense her: Earth. Ready to face the Shade Mages, Daphne tried using her Marinix powers to defend herself, but the curse placed over it turned her powers against her and rendered her as nothing more than a spirit without a body, unable to help defend her home.

In Cycle 5, the Aurora meet with Faragonda to discuss their newfound knowledge of their Veridix powers being less effective underwater, and they learn that they must acquire Marinix in order to defeat Tritannus. Faragonda tells them that Marinix is an ancient power that was born of the magic of the oceans, and that no one has acquired Marinix for a long time.

She informs that in order to begin their quest, they must find the Marinix Tome, but the only person who knows its location is Daphne, Lyra Vale's sister and the last aether to acquire Marinix. When Lyra Vale asks Daphne about the location of the Marinix Tome, Daphne tells her not to look for it because it is dangerous. Lyra Vale ends up persuading Daphne, who reveals that the Marinix Tome is in the Magic Archive of Aetheris Academy, despite the warning that the Marinix was cursed, and could bring about the end of the Aurora.

In Cycle 8, it is revealed that due to the magical ice on Dyamond, Marinix can adapt to the area's weather, changing its form into Crystal Marinix. According to Vexa Quill, it has a high degree of adaptability.

In order for a aether to earn Marinix, they are required to be able to undergo and pass all the riddles and trials to find and recover the three gems that represented and determined the three valued qualities of a fledgling aether's personality (Gem of Self-Confidence, Empathy, and Courage) in the waters of the various underwater realms of the Aether Realm within the duration of a single lunar cycle. After that, they need to activate the magic spring of Lake Roccaluce by using the Source of Marinix prior to the return of the following full moon. Due to the curse, if a aether does not complete the Marinix Quest within the duration of a single lunar cycle, they will be stripped of their aether powers permanently.

It is unknown if a shade mage can earn Marinix by completing Marinix Quest or not as it is a aether power. In the Nyx Coven's case, after Tritannus forced Daphne to tell him where the source of Marinix power was, Tritannus headed to Lake Roccaluce to destroy the Source of Marinix, so her Marinix cannot be protected from being stolen by him. After he destroyed the source, Tritannus absorbed Daphne's Marinix powers and used his trident to grant the Nyx Coven Marinix powers, which were mixed with his pollution powers.

The Marinix Clues were riddles given to the Aurora to find the gems of Self-Confidence, Empathy, and Courage.

Marinix grants its possessor access to the Endless Deep and gives aethers and shade mages the ability to use their magic more easily and freely in midair, terrestrial, and underwater conditions. It also helps them to swim faster, and breathe both in underwater, midair, and terrestrial conditions. In the comic series, Marinix grants the ability to communicate with marine life.

The Marinix possessors could also access the power of Chrono Magic to view certain events of the past in the Room of Faraway Reflections

In Cycle 8, it is revealed that their Crystal Marinix power grants them another ability, the Crystal Marinix light, which allows them to see the darkest part of the ocean. Their Crystal Marinix power also protects users from the cold.
