# Lumenix

Lumenix is a Aether Form introduced in Cycle 3.

In Cycle 3, Faragonda introduces Lumenix to the Aetheris Academy aethers upon their return from spring break. Lumenix is the "Final Aether Form" in a aether's formal educational journey to become a full-fledged aether. In Aetheris Academy's curriculum, earning Lumenix is considered their final exam. Lumenix aethers who graduate from Aetheris Academy are also the Guardian Aethers of their respective homeworlds, bound to protect their worlds from evil and destruction.

Lumenix aethers wear flowing clothes and soleless sandals. They have big, colorful Aether Dust-covered wings that have jewels hanging off of them. Their outfits are brightly colored and sometimes intricate. They wear barefoot sandals decorated with jewels that sometimes go up to their calves. Individually, hair may change color and/or grow. The hair is often decorated, braided, beaded, etc. Aethers often wear a tiara or other elaborate jewelry, and either a choker or matinee necklace that holds a bottle of Aether Dust. The form also comes with sparkling long gloves that almost reach the shoulders.

The outfits still consist of flowing clothes and soleless sandals. The outfits now cover their midriffs and gain some changes. The hairstyles are relatively unchanged. The wings remain unchanged (with the exception of Vexa Quill who has lower wings now), instead being recolored to match the aethers' theme colors; they are also no longer transluscent. The aethers still wear a tiara or other elaborate jewelry, and a choker. The form also retains its sparkling long gloves that almost reach the shoulders.

In order for a aether to earn Lumenix, she must rescue someone from her homeworld and show great sacrifice in doing so.

Vexa Quill and Lyra Vale are the only known exceptions to this rule. Vexa Quill does not save anyone specifically from her own realm; she saves the realm of Thalor by closing the Omega Portal. However, it is assumed that Vexa Quill does save the entire universe, which encompasses her homeworld, by sacrificing herself to close the portal on the assumption that unstoppable evil would ensue had she not.

Lyra Vale, by believing in her power to defeat evil (after meditating with the sorceress Maia on Pyros) is able to will herself into Lumenix. Lyra Vale would not have gotten her Lumenix if not for her inner dragon, Buddy, who had shown up prior to Lyra Vale meeting Maia. Her unconventional method of earning Lumenix results in it being incomplete, meaning she cannot use her miniaturization powers and can easily lose control.

Along with an increase in magical strength and endurance, Lumenix allows aethers to use Aether Dust to miniaturize, break dark spells, and various other things. Lumenix wings are endurable at higher wind speeds than those of a common aether. Lumenix also allows aethers to achieve Higher Aether Forms such as Veridix.
