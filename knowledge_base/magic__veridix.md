# Veridix

Veridix is a Aether Form introduced in Cycle 4. This form can be acquired without requiring the previous ascensions (Charmara and Lumenix).

In Cycle 4, Faragonda brings the Aurora into the Hall of Enchantments and informs them that after Lumenix, there is an infinite number of levels a aether can reach, the one above Lumenix being Veridix, a Aether Form that can affect the feelings of people. The Aurora soon learn that their Lumenix is ineffective against the Arcane Keepers of the Obsidian Ring, and that only Veridix can fight their evil power.

The outfits are casual, fitting the Earth theme. They mainly consist of a stylized skirt and/or capri with knee-high socks and boots or sandals. The wings are big and their design relate to power or theme. Aethers also obtain a type of wrist or arm accessory.

In order for a aether to achieve Veridix, they must make people believe in the existence of aethers and magic, thus Veridix can only be earned on Earth or somewhere where people do not believe in magic. Veridix can channel more mystical energy with the increasing faith in the existence of magic and aethers, which means that if a Veridix aether does not continue to convince people to believe in magic, their powers will weaken.

Luma Fern is the only known exception to this rule. She earned her Veridix by becoming angered at Gantlos and focusing her love for her dog, Artu, not by making people believe in magic. She also did not get Speedix, Zoomix, or Tracix.

It is implied that Veridix is a basic aether form for the Earth Aethers, as they all drew their power from the people's belief in their existence.

Veridix enables a aether to achieve the heart of the people and their feelings. It gives the aether new energy and the power to recover whoever is in need, and makes a aether more powerful with each person who believes in them. Veridix comes with four types of wings: the standard wings, and Speedix, Zoomix, and Tracix wings.

With Veridix, a aether has the power to reach out to someone's heart and help people overcome their bad habits or weaknesses. Every girl has a special effect added to their special spell, which is miniature versions of their Veridix wings.

Luma Fern's Special Veridix Power is unknown.

Every girl has a special effect added to their attacks, which is different for every girl and has something to do with their main power.

The power of Veridix is ineffective against the Major Aethers of Earth as they are the Supreme Guardians of Nature. Therefore, the Aurora needed stronger powers to face the Major Aethers and were given the Gifts of Destiny, the evolution of the Veridix power, by the Ethereal Aethers. The Gifts of Destiny provide Veridix aethers with two sub-ascensions: Sylvarix, which allows them to unite their magic with the essence of nature in Diana's kingdom, and Glacelix, which helps them in the cold regions of the North where Aurora rules. The last Gift of Destiny, the Black Gift, is not a ascension, but a spell that can bring someone back from dead.
