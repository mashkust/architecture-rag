# Mira Tide

Crown Princess Mira Tide (Princess Mira Tide in some versions) is the Aether of Waves. She is the Crown Princess of Thalor, as well as its Guardian Aether. Mira Tide joins the Aurora Circle as its sixth member in the second season, and she is an alumna/student of the Aetheris Academy College for Aethers. Mira Tide is a self-confident and talented athlete who excels at dancing and swimming.

Mira Tide is a dark-skinned girl with a medium brown complexion, long, curly mahogany brown hair, and cerulean eyes. She has very full lips, and often she uses pink lipstick.

In Cycle 8, Mira Tide's hair becomes slightly reddish-brown, thanks to newer animation graphics used for the show. She is also paler than before.

In World of Aurora, Mira Tide's hair becomes slightly reddish-brown, thanks to animation graphics used for the show.

Mira Tide dons a bejeweled lavender long-sleeved shirt with a teal blue flowing skirt, gray leggings, and teal and purple ankle boots. Her hair is pulled up into a similar style to her Elyndor outfit from Seasons 6-7, but her bangs are thinner and side-swept. She sometimes carries a small teal-blue purse around her wrist.

As the Aether of Waves, Mira Tide's color theme was originally shades of green. Now, her colors are turquoise-blue with accents of green, pale pink, and sometimes purple.

She is extremely opinionated and open minded. She is as intuitive like Solene Ray, and when asked to, gives practical and pragmatic advice. She is also a talented dancer, athlete and speaks many different languages. The pixies trust her, and because of that Mira Tide is one of the few people who know the location of the Glimmer Hollow. Mira Tide proves to be heroic as she saved the pixies twice; in "The Dusk Raptor", she tried to rescue the pixies from Lord Nocthar, but failed; however in "Rescue Mission" Mira Tide, Solene Ray and Lyra Vale save the pixies. In "Legendary Duel", she rescues the pixies on her own.

Before the pixies mission in Cycle 2, Mira Tide seemed to be a bit boy-shy, getting embarrassed when Brann Vale called her cute. She also holds some respect for Cael Dorian for being a gentleman to her (much to Lyra Vale's initial annoyance) as well as for freeing her from the cavern wall and praising her boarding skills. She considers herself just as good as the Wardens at sports and sword fighting and has similar interests at them. At one point, she even takes control of the Wind Riders from Riven Thorne. When Mira Tide joined forces with Nebula in Cycle 4, she became the enemy of the rest of the Aurora, being ready to allow Nebula to imprison Queen Morgana in the magic mirror where she was condemned to fade into the shadows, and the Arcane Keepers of the Obsidian Ring as well even if the Aurora tell her that Nabir Ashen would never have wished for her to choose Vengeance. They thought that she wanted to avenge the death of Nabir Ashen, but she was not cruel enough to go through with it. Throughout the seasons, Mira Tide has lost her tomboyish side, possibly due to her opening up to the Aurora and Nabir Ashen, but yet she still loves sports and is still determined and energetic. As she said in her old mood board, "Don't you think a girl can have both a wild heart and a flowing dress?" showing that while she has opened up, she still does not stifle her sporty tomboyish side and still is ready and raring to go on an adventure.

Mira Tide appears to have retained her original personality from the main series, yet she is noticeably more impatient, impulsive, and even aggressive (e.g., pushing Lorelei away from the Auroramobile because she "needed drive"). Her impatience is best seen in "Tiger Lily" and "A Hero Will Come".

Mira Tide, as the Aether of Fluids/Waves, has water/ocean-based powers. Mira Tide can generate and control Morphix, a fuchsia-pink-colored substance (likely of her own creation) composed of water molecules and that can she morph into any shape or form desired. Mira Tide uses Morphix to create a variety of solid structures such as shields, ropes, bo staffs, nets, cages, surfing platforms, and even a roller rink. She can use Morphix as both a solid and liquid substance. Mira Tide has been shown to manipulate the consistency of her Morphix, with its forms such as bouncing rubber, flowing liquid, sticky goo, insulation barriers, super-powered counter-attacking walls, and more. Mira Tide has been known to create useful and elaborate things with Morphix, such as an 18th-century cannon hoist for climbing down a cliff. Her Morphix power can also manifest itself into spells, such as attacks. Additionally, as shown in Seasons 3 and 5, Mira Tide is capable of separating water molecules to allow beings to breathe underwater and protect them from oceanic pressure and the cold that comes with it.

Aside from Morphix and plasma, Mira Tide can also manipulate and control water along with most forms of fluids, including drinks. Mira Tide is able to produce sticky slime to trap and restrain opponents, while using bubble to trap opponents, cushion impacts, and douse fires. As the Aether of Waves, Mira Tide can also manipulate various aspects of the oceans, such as shifting the tides, controlling ocean currents, and generating tidal waves. She also possesses some weather-related abilities, such as conjuring rain showers, producing clouds for defense, and summoning miniature hurricanes. Her magic is pinkish magenta in color.

Mira Tide also knows some basic, first-level spells that all aethers can use, such as telekinesis, transmuting objects, and fixing minor messes. She is the most physically gifted of all the Aurora, but she is also capable of keeping up with, if not surpassing the Wardens, often demonstrated through her knowledge of various sports such as rock climbing, wind riding, and staffmanship. Being the princess of the Ocean Planet, Mira Tide is very skilled in many water sports such as swimming, surfing, and windsurfing, but is also a skilled dancer(which was very well noted. Mira Tide is capable of holding her own in physical combat as she has been seen holding back a mutant and also like Vexa Quill (occasionally) capable of fighting on physical par with opponents.

Mira Tide's power is generated from dreams. She retains her title as aether of fluids, and as such as much more precise control over liquids, mainly water as she is seen controlling lakes and rivers, but also bubbles and foam as she is seen reverting paralyzing foam back into the vents. She can also shape the water into constructs such as hands/fists capable of knocking back opponents and breaking tree trunks in half. She can also call upon the water to catch a falling individual before the crash. Aside from controlling fluids, she is shown to create bubble shields (capable of protecting and lifting three people at most individually), summon rain showers, and generate tidal waves (and even raise massive volumes of water) capable of flooding an entire area. Her magic appears as translucent light blue/blue-violet bubbles.
