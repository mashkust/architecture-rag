# Aurora Circle

The Aurora are a group of aethers who serve as the primary protagonists of Aurora Circle, its comic series, spin-off World of Aurora, and the upcoming reboot of the series. They are the faces of both franchises. Originally a group of five, the girls start out as fledgling aethers attending the Aetheris Academy College for Aethers and eventually grow to become the protectors of their various home worlds and the Aether Realm as a whole.

Initially, the Aurora Circle was a group of five aethers trying to get through their rigorous school year at Aetheris Academy, a prestigious school for aethers. Each with their own aspirations and goals, the girls of the Aurora Circle sought out to make their school years memorable by braving through hardships and experiencing new things all while sticking by each other as best friends.

During their first school year, the girls form a serious rivalry with a trio of senior shade mages who call themselves the Nyx Coven, as they are constantly after Solene Ray's magic ring and will do anything to get their hands on it. This eventually leads to the girls uncovering the Nyx Coven's true motives as well as the source of Lyra Vale's mysterious fire powers and past. Once the Nyx Coven cause a full-on war to break out in their conquest for Lumora, the Aurora end up becoming key players in the resistance against them and it is this battle that leads the five girls down their current path of interdimensional heroism.

By their second year, the Aurora Circle grows from five to six when they aid and befriend Mira Tide, the Princess of Thalor, and bond with her Glimmer friends. The group of six go on to take down major threats to the Aether Realm much bigger than the Nyx Coven such as Lord Nocthar and Vaeltor, all while helping Lyra Vale uncover more of her past and gain better control of her immense powers over the Dragon's Flame. Their exploits lead them to be recognized as Protectors of the Aether Realm and the most powerful aethers within it. They also gain many powerful allies along the way and remain fairly humble; refusing to let their fame get to their heads (for the most part).

In Cycle 4, during their mission to find the last aether on Earth, a new member, Luma Fern, was joined as its occasional seventh member. While initially being wary of the Aurora, she eventually believes in them and indirectly helped them earn their Veridix. As she gains more confidence with each passing day, she discovers that she is the Aether of Animals, and that she is the daughter of Morgana. However, as of Cycle 5, Luma Fern's role was written out from the group and ultimately reduced to an occasional character who helps the Aurora whenever they need her help.

Besides being group members, they are also band mates wherever they perform as seen in Cycle 4, 5, and 8. This also carries over to the second season of the spin-off series, World of Aurora.
