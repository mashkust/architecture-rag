# Nyx Coven

The Nyx Coven are a trio of shade mages who act as the main antagonists of the Aurora Circle series. The group is made up of three direct descendants of the Primeval Shadows, the most powerful and evil shade mages to have ever existed. They focus on what they are best at: acting maliciously and joining forces with the darkest villains the Aether Realm has to offer.

The Nyx Coven were initially presented as a trio of senior shade mages attending the Noctis Spire School for Shade Mages, where they were idolized and feared by both their classmates and even some professors. The three shade mages reveled in having such a high reputation within Noctis Spire as they could practically make their classmates into their own minions and their high marks made them untouchable to their professors with the exception of the school's headmistress, Griffin.

At first, the Nyx Coven sought after a magic ring under the ownership of Princess Solene Ray of Helion, often using the tricks learned from their classes in their attempts to get it and, later, to torture the Aurora Circle for being constant thorns in their sides. After finding out that Solene Ray's magic ring was not the source of this "great power" they were after, the Nyx Coven soon set their sights on Lyra Vale after learning that she was the one in possession of it. Upon stealing this power—the Dragon's Flame—the Nyx Coven were revealed to be the descendants of The Ancestresses: the mothers of all shade mages and the ones responsible for the downfall of Elyndor, Lyra Vale's true homeworld. Believing it to be their destiny, the Nyx Coven abused the Dragon's Flame to plunge Lumora into a war as they conquered and destroyed everything in the realm with the exception of Aetheris Academy, which they had planned to save for last. Unfortunately for them, the Nyx Coven were defeated by the Aurora after Lyra Vale regained her lost powers, and were thrown into exile at the Fortress of Light.

Their failed conquest for Lumora leads them to constantly try to regain the power they once held and use it to take over the entirety of the Aether Realm. In later years, the three shade mages would ally themselves with greater forces of evil if it meant that they could achieve total domination of the Dimension; evil forces like Lord Nocthar, Vaeltor, and Tritannus. As they strengthened their own dark powers, the Nyx Coven would go back to employing minions to fight under them. Many would start off unwillingly but would later work under the Nyx Coven without hesitation after they make the false promise of sharing the power they plan to gain during their various conquests with these new minions. The most notable case of this would be when the Nyx Coven take over Noctis Spire a second time in Cycle 6.
