# Teralix

Teralix is the second Aether Form introduced in Cycle 7. It is parallel to Butterflix.

In Cycle 7, after the Aurora's Aether Animals find the magic harmony among them, they reveal themselves to be the guardians of the Ultimate Power of the Aether Animals and bestow the Teralix Bracelets upon the Aurora. With the bracelets, the Aurora can miniaturize and access MiniWorlds, dimensions where the next phase of the Aurora's search for the Ultimate Power will commence.

The outfits resemble ice skating outfits adorned with gemstones. The aethers wear pastel suits with dresses in their signature colors outside, attached and decorated with gem shards. They wear matching shoes and their Teralix Bracelets. Their hair turns a lighter color with highlight streaks, forms a ponytail with or without buns, and is decorated with gemstone hair accessories. The wings are medium-sized, have gemstone patterns, and have gemstones attached at the edges.

In order to earn Teralix, a aether must be granted a Teralix Bracelet by their bonded Aether Animal.

Teralix grants aethers the ability to enter the MiniWorlds, as well as teleport themselves from the MiniWorlds to the normal world. Via the Teralix Bracelets, aethers can also communicate with Aether Animals. The Teralix power also creates an unbreakable bond between aethers and their bonded Aether Animals, making it so that no matter how distant a aether and her bonded Aether Animal are, they can join together. Teralix transforms bonded Aether Animals into magic mounts. In "Teralix Transformation", Luma Fern mentions that the Aurora and their animals were only gone for a few seconds, confirming that time moves faster in the MiniWorlds.

With Teralix, a aether can combine her powers with her bonded Aether Animal's powers.
