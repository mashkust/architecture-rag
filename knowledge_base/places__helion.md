# Helion

Helion is a planet of the Aether Realm and the home realm of Solene Ray.

Helion is the realm of the Sun, the Moon, and the Stars as well as possibly one of the brightest planets in the entire Aether Realm.

Helion is predominantly seen as an extremely sunny place where it never rains; the only exception being Vaeltor's intervention in the episode "Vaeltor's Mark." In fact, there is so little rain that Solene Ray had to explain what a raincoat was to her tailors.

Helion possesses at least three Suns, with the magical essence of the first and second Suns of Helion being kept within the Steeple of the Sun located and heavily guarded within the royal palace. As seen in "Queen for a Day," the alignment of the three Suns of Helion is a rare occurrence that the citizens of Helion celebrate.

In Cycle 8, it is revealed Helion is illuminated by Lumenia, a neighboring star, although it is yet unknown if Lumenia is one of the already known three Suns of Helion or another, fourth, star which has not been seen yet.

The King and Queen of Helion can command the Second Sun to shine its light on other realms and if the Second Sun were to weaken or die out, Helion's king also suffers or perishes along with it as shown in "The Pillar of Light" .

As seen in "The Pillar of Light", Helion possesses at least one moon.

In the Aurora Circle Comic Series, the Second Sun even acts as a vital source of light to most, if not all, of the realms within the Aether Realm according to Morgana.

Like many realms of the Aether Realm, Helion is ruled by monarchy with King Radius as its ruler. No queen rules with him by his side after he and Luna divorced one another, leading to her stepping down as Queen. Helion has a royal guard whose members wear bright multicolored uniforms and use magic halberds.

The Ring of Helion, a ring made of pure light and a spark of the Dragon's Flame, is passed down through the royal family and has been since the beginning of Helion's history.

One notable holiday on Helion is the day in which the realm's three suns align perfectly in the sky. When this occurs, the current ruling monarch temporarily abdicates the throne to their successor, allowing them to act as king or queen for that whole day.

The comics also showcase other aspects of the lives of Helionics, such as how the realm's has a national sport: Magic Lights. As shown in Issue 150, Helionic children regularly play with balls of light, but the Magic Lights sport is so reputable to the people that it is akin to the Olympics on Earth. Yearly competitions called the Launching of the Magic Lights are held and the winner, out of four finalists, is naturally crowned as that year's champion.

Issue 130 shows a holiday other than the day of the three suns' alignment, which is the anniversary of its current monarch's (in that case, Radius') ascent to the throne.

There are doves that King Radius is quite fond of, and lets them fly free in a special room in the palace. Solene Ray also had a fox when she was little. Royal guards use black colored hounds. After Vaeltor took the Sun of Helion, a cavern Solene Ray used when she was a child was inhabitant by spiders and leeches.

In Royal Deeps, there are Bollabies and jellyfishes. One of these jellyfishes was mutated into monster by Tritannus. In the Royal Deeps, there is also an Ocean Gate between Helion and Thalor which is guarded by its very own Selkie, Illiris.

Helion is assumed to be a somewhat arid planet due to the lack of rain despite the presence of flourishing gardens at the royal palace. However, since the sun of Helion is magical (which is why Vaeltor stole its light), it is possible that it causes a flourishing flora to grow there, as pine forests can be seen around the palace. Helion may have more than one moon just like it has more than one sun.

Land around Royal Deeps seems to be more tropical like and Deeps itself contains coral reefs of Helion.

There are also large, colorful crystals sticking up out of the earth in many lands around Helion and the palace.
