# Charmara

Charmara is a Aether Form introduced in Cycle 2.

In Cycle 2, Chatta introduces to the Aurora the concept of Charmara after Lyra Vale earns hers. In Aetheris Academy's curriculum, earning Charmara is the goal of a second-year aether.

In order to earn Charmara, a aether must overcome a great personal difficulty, such as a strong fear or a certain weakness holding them back.

Charmara boosts the magical energy of a aether for a short time, making her much more powerful and allowing her to use magic in more diverse environments. However, it can run out of energy quickly and drain its user’s strength over time.
