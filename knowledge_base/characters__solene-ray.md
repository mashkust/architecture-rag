# Solene Ray

Crown Princess Solene Ray is the Aether of the Sun and Moon, or the Aether of the Shining Sun. She is a creative, skilled artist who is Brann Vale's fiancée. Solene Ray is Helion's Guardian Aether, as well as the sole heiress to the Helionic Royal Throne, signified by her status as keeper of the royal family ring. She is one of the founding members of the Aurora Circle and is an almuna/student of the Aetheris Academy College for Aethers. Though immature and even selfish and bossy at times, Solene Ray cares for her fellow Aurora and sees herself responsible for them all as sort of a big sisterly figure.

Solene Ray is a lightly tan-skinned young girl with long blonde hair that stops below her waist and golden amber eyes.

Solene Ray's civilian look for both seasons of World of Aurora consists of an ombre pink short-sleeve top over a white dress shirt with a light blue floral necklace, silver elbow-length fingerless gloves that are covered in pink sparkles, a light blue skirt, silver thigh-high socks that are covered in pink sparkles (much like her gloves), and pink open-toed high heels with tiny silver bows just above the opening. Occasionally, she can be seen wearing a small pink handbag with black beaded straps.

Holding true to her many aether titles as well as her name, Solene Ray's Aether Forms maintain a theme of light; mainly that of stars. Orange stands out as the primary color of a majority of her forms, with purples and pinks acting as accents.

Although Solene Ray is usually a proud and self-centered girl, she also cares deeply about her friends and family, not just herself. She will stick to the people she loves and do anything to keep them safe. Solene Ray is not always seen wearing a smile on her face, but she does crack jokes to cheer her friends up when they are sad. Essentially, Solene Ray has a cheerful and optimistic personality.

Besides that, she is totally obsessed with fashion; which clothes look best on her, and which dresses match her face the best. Solene Ray often gives fashion advice to people in Aetheris Academy, but this advice is often not taken well. She would rather flirt with Brann Vale than study and do work, which sometimes annoys the other girls. The most intuitive of all the members, Solene Ray likes to match make but has often had problems with understanding why her friends do not always like getting set up.

However, despite her intuitions, Solene Ray can be rather oblivious at times. She was sent to Aetheris Academy to become a stronger aether, but this did not mean so much to her, though she did effectively become a fully accomplished aether and a powerful one by fighting the first three season villains with the Aurora. She uses her magic for anything, but she mostly wants to help her friends as deep inside she is a generous and helpful girl. Although she might disagree with her friends, Solene Ray is very supportive and protective and will not hesitate to help them. She learns to accept that love and friendship are far more important than royalty, fashion, and shopping. Sometimes she hides her true personality because of her upbringing. In short, her cheerful exterior masks great sorrow.

Solene Ray, as the Aether of the Shining Sun, Moon and Stars, she has Sun/Star-based and Moon-based powers. Solene Ray can mainly control light and heat, which can be explained by her connection to the Sun. She can throw blasts of light energy and can make balls of solar heat, which occasionally have the ability to burns things. She can concentrate her energy to make herself glow brightly in dark places along with producing an orbiting light sphere to illuminate dark areas or even producing "spotlights". Solene Ray is able to create explosions of sunlight that can leave the opponent temporarily blind as demonstrated in "Face to Face with the Enemy" and "The Arcane Keepers' Attack". She can shape light into barriers and shields, which as seem in "Nebula" will produce bright flashes when making contact with a physical force. She demonstrated the ability of controlling colors in A Lumora Christmas". Due to Solene Ray being from Helion, she is majorly affected by the Sun. She gains her energy from the sun and is heavily weakened when in very dark places or underground. Solene Ray has displayed her moon magic more prominently in later seasons often as magic mirror spells such as summoning mirrors to deflect attacks, conjuring crystal shards, and shifting the reflections of her own and others to make their appearance similar to others, as demonstrated in "Mira Tide's Choice". Her magic is yellow in color.

She has the Ring of Helion which allows for her focus on her energy allowing it to be used as a light source. Solene Ray is capable of teleporting herself and others in a flash of light as demonstrated in "An Unexpected Event", where she teleports herself, Lyra Vale, Mike and Vanessa to Aetheris Academy with the latter being teleported back home. As, while she is seen using her scepter to teleport large groups de-transformed ("The Black Willow's Tears"), while transformed ("Fury!") the most people she can teleport all together is 9. As, seen in "At the Last Moment", she is able to teleport herself(while transformed) multiple times without tiring herself out. During the events of "Queen for a Day", she is able to summon a group of shade mages in a blinding flash of light as well as teleport the Aurora and herself to Zenora in "A Monster Crush", both without the use of her scepter and while de-transformed.

Solene Ray has the ultimate ability to manipulate solar, lunar, and stellar phenomenon as demonstrated with her anti-sunshine spell (essentially gravity wells). Her element of light, specializes in sight and clarity, giving others the ability to see both the path and truth in front of them. As seen in "Nebula", she was able to sense Luma Fern's impurity when she was possessed by Nebula and in Cycle 7 Solene Ray's Butterflix Power is able to restore purity. Since her powers are derived from celestial objects she is able to manipulate cosmic forces. Solene Ray has also shown abilities of generating weather phenomena such as controlling clouds to either uncloak or produce rain showers, creating solar flares to melt ice and grow plants. She can also create stellar orbs.

Solene Ray also knows some basic, first-level spells that all aethers can use, such as telekinesis, transmuting objects, and fixing minor messes. Solene Ray is a very talented fashion designer as she is often seen conjuring various fabrics(as well as complete outfits) even utilizing this skill to open a fashion boutique inside her dorm room.
