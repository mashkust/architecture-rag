# Roxy

Crown Princess Roxy is the Fairy of Animals. For a long time, she did not even suspect the existence of magic nor sought it out, but when the Wizards of the Black Circle attempted to kidnap her, the Winx came to her rescue, revealing her true identity as a fairy. Although Roxy initially resisted the change, she eventually adapted, developed her magical abilities, and after Gantlos injured Artu, she received the Believix transformation, allowing her to confront her enemies. Roxy and the Winx, whom she joined as the seventh and youngest member, were able to free the Earth Fairies, and Roxy was able to reunite with her mother Morgana, whom she had brought to Gardenia to live with Klaus, Roxy's father. After this, Roxy finally decided to enroll in Alfea, and her application was approved. Faragonda introduces her to the other students as a key element in bringing back the people of Earth's belief in fairies and magic in general.

Roxy is a young girl with a slightly light skin tone. She has waist-length, pinkish-purple hair with blonde tips and dark violet eyes.

She wears a sea green, long-sleeve, skin-tight cropped shirt with a hot pink cropped shirt over it which is shorter and with a hole on each shoulder, and has a lavender paw print in front. She wears short, denim capris with a metal chain hanging off at her waist and hot pink boots with light purple laces and soles.

Roxy's Believix consists of a light sea green, one-armed top that has two pale lavender and light pink straps holding it over her right shoulder. She also has light sea green shorts with greenish-yellow ruffles underneath and a greenish-yellow belt over them. The belt has three purple circles on it, with several pink ruffles hanging off of it. On her right arm is a light sea green, fingerless glove that reaches between her shoulder and elbow. Her shoes are lime green, platform boots with white heels and pink socks. Her hair does not change. Her wings are edged with green and dark purple on the inside. At the edges of the wings are swirled, light pink designs.

Roxy is rebellious, a little shy, and willful. She cares a great deal for animals, loving them and keeping them as pets to the point of making her own secret shelter for them in the back of her father's bar. She is very brave, having confronted the Black Circle a couple of times and burglars even when she could not transform, and is always willing to help those in need.

Roxy, as the Fairy of Animals, has animal-based powers. Roxy can empathize with animals and feel what they are feeling, and can calm them down if they are scared, hurt, or angry. She can speak to them and summon them telepathically. Roxy can also grant animals the ability to speak like humans. Roxy has also been shown to lend magical strength to them, as she did to Artu in "Winx Believix!", allowing him to break free of Gantlos and return to her side. Roxy also has the ability to read an animal's mind and make them act under her control allowing her to tame wild animals. Her magic is colored aquamarine/blue green.

Roxy can also make an animal out of inanimate objects, such as ice structures, and make them act under her command. Roxy seems to possess a form of "danger sense" as seen in "Winx Club Forever!", when she could sense the Wizards of the Black Circle's presence in her house. Roxy's spells can take on the forms animals such as a kaleidoscope (group of butterflies).

Roxy has been shown to use the White Circle to enhance her powers, such as using it to summon a larger than normal flock of creatures of the sky and forest, and make them surround the enemy under her command. As her powers grew throughout the series, Roxy has displayed the use of basic attack spells such as energy rays, and defensive spells such as shields. Roxy also knows some basic, first-level spells that all fairies can use, such as telekinesis, transmuting objects, and fixing minor messes.

Likely due to having magic in her body, Roxy possesses enhanced durability and strength. In the seventh and twelfth episodes of season 4, she was shown falling roughly twenty feet and being blasted through a metal wall without injury. Also during episode seven, she was shown easily flinging herself up and behind cover and carrying her roughly one hundred pound dog in her arms after his injury prevented him from walking.

In "The Golden Butterfly", it is revealed she can also sense the connection and its strength between an animal and person, which was demonstrated when she sensed the strong connection between Faragonda and her digmole.

Roxy maintains her status as the Fairy of Animals and thus, retains her animal-oriented powers. She uses her almost psychic-like connection with Artu to keep the detectives distracted, giving Bloom time to make her way to Annabelle's apartment.
