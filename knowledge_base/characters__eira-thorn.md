# Eira Thorn

Eira Thorn is the Aether of Nature. She hails from Verdalis, where she currently acts as its Guardian Aether. She is Helion Voss's girlfriend. The team usually relies on her for potions and magic advice. Eira Thorn is one of the founding members of the Aurora Circle, being the third Aurora girl introduced after Lyra Vale and Solene Ray, and is an alumna/student of the Aetheris Academy College for Aethers as of The Secret of the Lost Kingdom.

Eira Thorn is tan-skinned with long light-brown hair that has bangs framing on either side of her face where it has visible blonde streaks with a single small hair strand sticking up (it is sometimes unseen in both her civilian and aether forms) and narrow green-jade eyes.

Eira Thorn's civilian look for both seasons of World of Aurora consists of a small short-sleeved light blue hoodie with lighter blue flowers across the chest worn over a white dress shirt, a skirt in various shades of pink and white, sparkly silver leggings and strapply pink heels. She also wears six different bracelets on her right wrist and always has one long strand of hair hang off the right side of her face that often rests on her right shoulder. Occasionally, she can be seen sporting a small, square pink handbag/purse.

As the self-proclaimed Aether of Nature, many of Eira Thorn's Aether Forms retain a flowery theme to them; namely that of a pink rose. While pink stands as the signature color of Eira Thorn's various forms with green as an accent, some forms have the colors reversed with green as the primary color and pink as its accent.

Eira Thorn is shown to be a very calm, soft-spoken, caring, sweet, shy and genuine girl who loves plants and vegetation of all kinds. Being interested in experimenting with her plants, Eira Thorn became the potion master of the group who specializes in brewing and creating remedies and medicines from the uses of her magical plants. Due to her gentle and caring personality, Eira Thorn is strongly connected to nature and loves helping people. She is shown to have a sensitive personality to the point where she would instinctively care for her friends as well as her plants when they are in distress.

Eira Thorn is the most mature member of the Aurora as she possesses a deep sense of love, peace, happiness and tranquility. However, it is shown that Eira Thorn has an insecure side to her personality as she would have problems believing in herself at times. Her insecurity would sometimes cause her to hide most of her feelings to the point where she would have problems expressing them. Eira Thorn is one of the most diligent student at Aetheris Academy and is cautious of her actions. Although she has a kind-hearted side, she at times gets very angry at her enemies which can cause her to act quickly and show a more aggressive side of herself. This is seen a few times throughout the series.

As an older sister, she is shown to be quite overprotective who often forgets that people do mature and can protect themselves. Miele often has to deal with this side of Eira Thorn whenever the latter suspects a mission to be too dangerous. In this case, Eira Thorn has demonstrated some level of ignorance as this attitude tends to hurt Miele's feelings.

Eira Thorn, as the Aether of Nature, has nature-based powers. Eira Thorn can generate, grow, and control various vegetation, but she mainly uses vines for defensive moves such as creating barriers, entangling opponents, and supporting heavy objects. Aside from her vines, she is often seen controlling flowers to use as diverse attacks using pollen and dust to provide distractions, producing swirls of petals/leaves, conjuring food (namely fruits/vegetables), altering the growth plants such as growing a tomato crop from an already existing tomato ("Solene Ray's Big Party"), and even summoning animals ("Mission at Cloudtower").

Eira Thorn's strength relies on her surrounding environment and requires her to be at one with nature. Eira Thorn is able to hear the voice of nature speaking to her, more so than in an instinctive manner that all Aetheris Academy Aethers are taught. Rather, she is able to use her magic to listen to the voice of the forest. This skill has also proven to be a disadvantage at times, as the destruction of nearby nature will cause excruciating pain to her and weaken her own self and her ability to focus her magic properly, even her flight. Eira Thorn like Vexa Quill and Nyra Tune seems to have a very strong connection with her magical element and her natural essence, in her case she is like a plant seeing as her power were completely negated in the simulated cave in "Rescue Mission" and when Vaeltor used a spell that depleted her of light witnessed in "Fury". By Cycle 4, Eira Thorn develops the ability to alter the seasons which she promptly displays in a fight against the Snow Queen, by bringing forth a Spring thaw.

Eira Thorn in terms of elemental power represents earth which she displays by setting off earthquakes and smashing a boulder with her mind. Her source of power is life itself as she has been seen turning inanimate objects into living creatures such as ice arrows into birds, stuffed animals into the aether pets. She also has a connection with the air being capable of summoning powerful breezes, using plants to absorb smoke and produce oxygen, and producing fragrances that can be used to repeal an enemy or calm them down as seen in "Solene Ray's Big Party" and "Mission for the Prime Stars". Eira Thorn has developed the ability to physically heal other beings mostly after they have experienced a recent trauma. Her magic is green in color.

Like all magical beings Eira Thorn can utilizes magical energy based attacks and defenses. She also knows some basic first-level spells, such as telekinesis, transmuting objects and fixing minor messes. Eira Thorn is shown to be gifted with potion brewing as well as herbalism.

Eira Thorn still maintains her status as the Aether of Nature and thus, retains her nature-based oriented powers and draws power from the nature of her surroundings. She can conjure up vines to bind her enemies, which is even a specialty of hers granted by her Dreamix powers, rescue others if necessary, and release blasts of flower petals. However, her powers seem much more limited as, more often than not, her vines will fail to keep an enemy restricted.
