# Wardens

The Wardens is a group made up of many Crimson Forge Wardens. Initially starting out with four, they were brought together as a squad during their first year in Crimson Forge. They also serve as the secondary protagonists of Aurora Circle as they date and assist the titular group in their various adventures.

The group has undergone many member changes - gaining new members, senior members leaving on their own accord, or through a sudden absence, and even death. As a result, the Wardens have become one of the most actively changing groups in the series.

Initially, this group consisted of four: Cael Dorian, Brann Vale, Tym Quill and Riven Thorne, who were all brought together as a squad as they attended Crimson Forge. The boys faced many interpersonal struggles as they tried to learn the ropes of becoming strong warriors, which inadvertently helped them grow closer as a group as they forged true friendships and trust within each other, made memories and lived an adventurous first year of school.

Most of the group's struggles that year were the fruit of Riven Thorne's difficult behavior and eventual betrayal in his defecting to the Nyx Coven. However, he would later realize that his true value with the Nyx Coven was as their pawn, which would help him realize what true friendship was and who his real friends were.

By their second year, the group is joined by Helion Voss, a former Crimson Forge student who was later readmitted and nephew (or grandson) to Headmaster Saladin. They would also go on to assist the Aurora and their new friend, Mira Tide, in taking down Lord Nocthar as their reputation at Crimson Forge grew to the point of being considered one of the best squads it had to offer.

Over a year later, the group grows in size once more with the addition of Nabir Ashen, a skillful arcane keeper from Thalor but, unfortunately, he does not remain long as he later sacrifices himself for the safety and lives of the Earth Aethers in Cycle 4. Nabir Ashen's death/disappearance greatly impacts his friends, mainly by forcing them to realize that their various adventures could cost them their lives. Both his best friend, Riven Thorne, and fiancée, Mira Tide, were negatively impacted the most with the latter facing more difficulty with moving on.

The group is later joined by Roy, the royal yacht driver from Thalor, in Cycle 5 as they assist the Aurora in dealing with Tritannus, Mira Tide's psychotic cousin who wished to destroy his family for making his brother, Nereus, take what he believed he deserved: position as next-in-line to the Thalor Throne. By Cycle 6, Roy was admitted into Crimson Forge but inexplicably leaves the group towards the end of that very same season. Riven Thorne, one of the longest staying members, also leaves the group to further his training and better himself as a person following his breakup with Nyra Tune, but, by season eight, he has returned to his place on the squad. Additionally, Nex joins the group as a Paladin and becomes a student of Crimson Forge in season eight.
