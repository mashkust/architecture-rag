# Mythera

Mythera is the second Aether Form introduced in Cycle 6. It is parallel to Florenix.

In Cycle 6, the Aurora search for magical artifacts to lock the Myth Archive for good. In their search, they go to Tir Na Nog and attempt to acquire the Ancestral Wands. In doing so, they learn that long ago, seven Ancestral Aethers crossed over from the Myth Archive World to the real world and spread their magic throughout all the worlds of the Aether Realm, including the Earth. They brought with them seven Ancestral Wands, which contain the Mythera power, which allows entry into the Myth Archive World. However, one must not stay in the Myth Archive World for too long, or else they will become fictional characters, stuck in the pages of the Myth Archive forever.

The outfits are prom-like dresses and high heels or boots with ruffles and flowers in design. The outfits have bright color schemes and are not complicated. The boots are designed to match well with the outfits. The hair is tied, or curled, and decorated with a matching flower. Also, their wings are big and layered with sharp tips. Some of the aethers may gain tattoos.

In order to earn Mythera, a aether must prove herself before the Ancestral Wands. In return, the wand she receives will grant her Mythera.

Mythera gives its users the ability to enter the Myth Archive World and also the ability to teleport themselves in and out of the Myth Archive World. The Ancestral Wands grant their users the ability to summon projections of legendary clues by exclaiming "Mythera Insight" or "Mythera Vision". The wands are also used as a homing device for the Fantasy Emerald and the Silver Spear.

Every girl gains an elemental effect with respect to the aether's individual powers.
